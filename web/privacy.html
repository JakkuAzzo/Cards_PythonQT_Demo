PRIVACY · PRE-RELEASE

Built for the room, not a profile.

Cards v1 is local-first. It does not require an account, cloud save, advertising identifier, analytics profile, or online game service to play.

Information the app uses

When you choose nearby play, Cards uses Bluetooth, local-network, and camera permissions only to discover a nearby player or place an optional AR table. Game state stays on the participating devices. The app does not upload it to a Cards server because v1 has no Cards server.

Optional feedback

The feedback form on this site is processed by FormSubmit and delivered to the Cards support inbox. Send only the information needed to describe a problem or suggestion. Do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Local storage

Cards stores selected packs, favourites, local play statistics, and local preferences on the device so the app can restore your choices. Removing the app removes app-managed local data according to the operating system’s normal behaviour.

Changes

This page will be updated before a production store release if Cards adds cloud accounts, online multiplayer, analytics, crash reporting, or other data-processing services.
